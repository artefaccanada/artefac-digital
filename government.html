FAQ

Government Procurement FAQ

Are you a Canadian company?
Yes. ARTEFAC INC. is a Canadian company headquartered in Mississauga, Ontario, incorporated as an Ontario corporation and operating in Canada since 2004. ARTeFAC Digital is the operating name for our digital and AI automation services.
Do you work with municipalities?
Yes. We design AI workflow, citizen-service, document-processing and knowledge-management solutions for municipal environments. We can work directly with municipalities or as a subcontractor to a prime delivering municipal work.
Can solutions use Canadian-hosted infrastructure?
Yes. Depending on the cloud provider selected, the AI model chosen and the specific government requirement, Canadian data residency can be incorporated into the solution design. We discuss infrastructure options transparently with each client based on their specific privacy and security requirements.
Do you support human oversight of AI decisions?
Yes. Human approval and auditability can be incorporated into all AI workflows. We treat human oversight as a requirement for consequential decisions, not an optional add-on. All AI outputs that affect decisions, communications or actions are treated as drafts pending human review unless a specific low-risk workflow has been assessed and approved otherwise.
Can you work as a subcontractor to a larger government supplier?
Yes. We welcome subcontracting arrangements with established federal and provincial prime contractors requiring Canadian AI workflow automation, document processing, knowledge-assistant, data-analytics or apid proof-of-concept capabilities. Contact us to discuss teaming arrangements.
Do you support accessibility requirements?
Yes. Accessibility can be incorporated into design, development and testing requirements. We work toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA for web and digital interfaces, and apply CAN-ASC-6.2 principles for accessible and equitable AI. Our own website accessibility statement is available at artefac.digital/accessibility.
What is your procurement registration status?
We are in the process of completing supplier registration on CanadaBuys / SAP Business Network, and are preparing our submission for the PSPC Artificial Intelligence Source List, Band 1. If your procurement timeline requires formal registration, contact us early so we can confirm timing and provide any required supplier information.
