Delivery Experience

Relevant AI Delivery Experience

Documented implementations demonstrating applicable AI automation delivery capabilities. Commercial case studies are labeled accordingly.

Relevant Commercial AI Case Study

Healthcare Administration AI Dental Clinic New Patient Workflow

Challenge

Manual patient intake processing causing administrative bottlenecks and delayed new patient onboarding responses.

Scope

Automated intake form processing, triage classification and staff notification for a multi-location dental clinic.

Solution

AI document processing system with classification logic, automated patient communication and staff escalation workflow.

Architecture

API-based document extraction, classification engine, CRM integration, human review checkpoint before patient confirmation.

Human Oversight

Staff review and approval required before any patient-facing communication is sent.

Privacy Approach

Minimal data collection; no patient health information stored beyond immediate processing window; configurable retention.

Relevant Commercial AI Case Study

AI Knowledge Assistant Accounting Firm Client Communication

Challenge

High volume of repetitive client inquiries on tax deadlines, document requirements and filing status consuming professional staff time.

Scope

Source-based AI assistant using approved firm documentation to answer common client questions with citations.

Solution

Knowledge retrieval assistant grounded in approved firm documentation; all responses cite source document; staff escalation path for complex queries.

Architecture

Retrieval-augmented generation over approved document set; no free-form AI generation without source grounding; human escalation workflow.

Human Oversight

Complex or ambiguous queries automatically routed to professional staff; all AI outputs include source citation for review.

Relevant Commercial AI Case Study

Service Request Automation Trades & HVAC Service Triage

Challenge

Incoming service requests required manual categorization, priority assessment and dispatch creating delays during peak periods.

Scope

Automated intake triage, urgency classification, routing and appointment scheduling workflow.

Solution

AI triage system classifying incoming requests by urgency and type; routes to appropriate technician with relevant job history context.

Architecture

Natural language classification, business-rule engine, CRM integration, dispatcher review interface.

Human Oversight

All dispatch decisions presented to human dispatcher for confirmation before technician notification.

Result

Reduction in manual triage time; improved consistency of urgency classification; dispatcher confirms all assignments.

Procurement Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from procurement professionals and prime contractors.

Is ARTeFAC Digital a Canadian company?
Yes. ARTEFAC INC. is a Canadian corporation based in Mississauga, Ontario, operating since 2004. The company is 100% Canadian-owned.
Are you registered on CanadaBuys / SAP Business Network?
Yes. ARTEFAC INC. holds an active SAP Business Network / CanadaBuys registration, enabling participation in Government of Canada procurement processes.
What is your PSPC AI Source List status?
ARTeFAC Digital has submitted a Band 1 application under the PSPC Artificial Intelligence Source List (Solicitation No. WS4286933967). Formal qualification decisions rest with PSPC. We do not claim qualification before formal acceptance.
Do you work with municipalities?
Yes. We develop AI workflow, citizen-service, document-processing and knowledge-management solutions suitable for municipal environments, including 311 assistants, permit and licensing guides, document intake and internal knowledge tools.
Can solutions use Canadian-hosted infrastructure?
Yes, where supported by the selected cloud and AI provider and required by the project. We work with clients to identify data-residency requirements and select provider and deployment configurations accordingly.
Do you support human oversight for AI decisions?
Yes. Human approval and auditability can be incorporated into all AI workflows at defined decision points. We design systems with explicit human-in-the-loop steps for consequential decisions and maintain complete audit trails.
Can ARTeFAC Digital work as a subcontractor?
Yes. We welcome collaboration with established federal, provincial and municipal prime contractors requiring AI workflow automation, document intelligence, knowledge assistants, prototyping support or AI implementation expertise.
Do you support accessibility requirements?
Yes. Accessibility can be incorporated throughout requirements, design, implementation and testing. We can align with WCAG 2.1 AA, EN 301 549 procurement considerations and CAN-ASC-6.2 principles for accessible AI.
Do you work with different AI providers?
Yes. Our architecture can be designed around project-specific model, cloud, privacy, data-residency and integration requirements. We are not tied to a single AI provider and select technologies based on client requirements.
Do you support responsible AI reviews?
Yes. We can support risk documentation, AI governance processes, Algorithmic Impact Assessment support and implementation controls appropriate to project requirements. Responsible AI documentation is available on request.
Are you a certified or government-approved supplier?
ARTEFAC INC. holds an active CanadaBuys / SAP Business Network registration. We have submitted a Band 1 application to the PSPC AI Source List; formal qualification rests with PSPC. We do not claim government certification or approval beyond confirmed registrations.
